Yufei Shao is an undergraduate student in the Department of Biology at Nanjing University and is visiting Dong Laboratory at Yale. Her research interest lies in AI for genomics, especially developing methods for data integration and transcriptomics analysis. During her studies, she investigated epigenomic signals using nanopore sequencing data at Walter and Eliza Hall Institute of Medical Research (2025 summer), and developed an AI framework for spatial transcriptomics analysis in the Department of artificial intelligence at Nanjing University (2025-2026). She hopes to elucidate the molecular mechanisms of Parkinson's disease in Dong lab. In addition to this, Yufei enjoys hiking, badminton, and photography in her spare time.
